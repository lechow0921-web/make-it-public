政策PolicyUCL 硕士论文UCL dissertation2025 — 2026

日本阿伊努政策：写给游客的“尊重”Japan's Ainu policy: "respect" written for tourists

以为Assumed

文本上尊重原住民文化的政策，自然会惠及原住民。A policy that honours Indigenous culture on paper will benefit Indigenous people.

发现Found

政策被包装成面向外国游客的国家品牌资产；当事群体关于土地、渔猎与生计的诉求在决策中缺席。The policy was packaged as a nation-brand asset for foreign visitors; the community's claims on land, fishing and livelihood were absent from the decision.

话语分析Discourse analysis政策评估Policy evaluation利益相关方分析Stakeholder analysis
这对商业意味着什么Why it matters beyond policy

供给方用自己的预设定义了用户需求，方案做出来了，却没解决对方的真问题。用户研究的价值，就是在投入资源之前拦下这样的方案。研究基于政策文本与公开资料。The provider defined the need by its own assumptions; the product shipped and solved the wrong problem. User research earns its keep by stopping that plan before money is spent. Based on policy texts and public sources.

18 秒的东京夜景，跑赢了所有攻略An 18-second Tokyo night clip beat every guide I made

以为Assumed

信息越完备、投入越高的攻略，越受欢迎。The more complete and effortful the guide, the better it performs.

发现Found

在供给饱和的品类里，稀缺性比信息完备更重要：小众机位、高完播的形式，加上匹配的受众结构，更容易带来收藏和分享。In a saturated category, scarcity beats completeness: a rare vantage point plus a high-completion format, matched to the audience, drives saves and shares.

局限与验证设计Limits & test design

一条爆款推不出规律，偶然因素要和内容因素分开。验证方法：控制内容形式不变，分别发布小众选题和热门选题两组样本，再比较收藏率与分享率。One hit is not a rule; luck has to be separated from content. The test: hold format constant, publish a niche-topic set and a popular-topic set, then compare save and share rates.

产品Product小红书搜索页 AI 摘要 · 行为观察RED search AI summary · field notes待验证Hypothesis

来小红书，不是为了一个光滑的答案People don't come to RED for a smooth answer

以为Assumed

用户搜索，是想更快得到一个答案。Searchers want one faster answer.

发现Found

在依赖个人体验的问题上，用户要的是真人经验和分歧本身。把多篇笔记压成一个结论，削弱了社区区别于搜索引擎的那部分价值。For experience-dependent questions, people want real accounts and the disagreement itself. Compressing many notes into one verdict erodes what sets a community apart from a search engine.

§ 4方法Method

一个循环，三种证据One loop, three kinds of evidence

我没有“用户研究员”的岗位标签，但方法是通的。公共政策和用研的内核一样：定义问题、识别利益相关方、调研真实需求、评估影响、迭代。区别只在于对象从公民变成了用户。I haven't held a "user researcher" title, but the method transfers. Public policy and user research share a core: define the problem, map stakeholders, research real needs, assess impact, iterate. Only the subject changes, from citizens to users.

硕士论文 · UCLMSc dissertation · UCL

国家品牌与原住民政策：日本阿伊努案例Nation branding and Indigenous policy: the Ainu case

以国家品牌理论为框架，用话语分析研究日本政府如何把阿伊努政策建构为面向全球受众的叙事，以及对内实质与对外传播之间的落差如何损耗公信力。Using nation-branding theory and discourse analysis, it examines how Japan framed its Ainu policy for global audiences, and how the gap between domestic substance and outward messaging erodes credibility.

治理、营销和原住民研究一样，都需要尊重对象的主体性和诉求。Governance and marketing, like Indigenous studies, have to respect the agency and claims of the people they address.
